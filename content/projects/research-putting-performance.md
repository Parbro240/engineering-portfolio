## Research Overview

I've started putter face research under Dr. Xiang at Oklahoma State University. I want to understand how different face materials and surface finishes affect what a golf ball does immediately after impact.

I'm interested in the ball's bounce, skid, spin, and transition to true roll. This research builds on my work designing and prototyping custom putters.

## What I Want to Compare

- How different face materials affect the ball's initial motion.
- How surface finishes change the ball's behavior after contact.
- How those differences relate to bounce, skid, spin, and true roll.

## Possible Test Methods

High-speed video and ball tracking could help me study the ball's motion. Marked golf balls could help track rotation.

A controlled putting or pendulum-style setup could allow comparisons between face materials and surface finishes.

## Ongoing Work

As the research progresses, I'll use this page to share the test setup, measurements, and findings.
