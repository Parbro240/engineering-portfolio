## Research question

How do putter face materials and surface finishes affect golf-ball behavior after impact?

I am pursuing undergraduate research into bounce, skid, spin, and the transition to true roll. This direction builds on my interest in custom putter design and physical prototyping.

## Variables of interest

- **Face material:** compare how different contact materials affect initial ball behavior.
- **Surface finish:** examine the effect of different face surface conditions.
- **Ball motion:** observe bounce, skid, spin, and the transition to true roll after impact.

## Methods under consideration

The research may use high-speed video, ball tracking, and marked golf balls. A controlled putting or pendulum-style setup is also under consideration for comparing face materials or surface conditions.

These are possible methods, not a description of completed experiments or an established test apparatus.

## Current status

The research is in development. No completed experiments, published results, or measured material comparisons are presented here.

As the work develops, this case study can include the finalized test setup, experimental controls, ball-tracking approach, measured results, and limitations. Conclusions will be added only when supported by data.
